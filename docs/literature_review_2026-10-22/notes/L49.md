# L49 — Input-to-State Safety With Control Barrier Functions

Shishir Kolathaya, Aaron D. Ames; printed DOI 10.1109/LCSYS.2018.2853698; arXiv:1803.03035v3, 11 August 2018. All PDF prose read; the earlier p. 3 truncation has been repaired by a separate page read. Original formulas/figures and independent proof verification deferred.

**Task:** characterize bounded additive input perturbations using invariant **enlarged** safe sets, not strict invariance of the original set. ISSf-CBF construction adds control authority along the barrier gradient, with class-K disturbance-to-set enlargement (pp. 3–5, Theorems 1–2). CLF is relaxed while barrier stays hard in the ISSf-QP; this does not turn enlarged-set safety into zero violation of the original constraint.

**Evidence:** scalar examples and simulated two-DOF mechanical system, varying disturbance/gain (pp. 5–6); not local-map reconstruction or robot transport experiments.

**DBACT use:** quantify how residual/tracking error consumes safety margin, and keep exact clearance, practical neighborhood and probabilistic guarantees distinct. This is theoretical background, not a task baseline or proof that unknown boundary gaps are bounded.
