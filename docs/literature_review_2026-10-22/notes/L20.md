# L20 — Obstacle Avoidance for Unicycle-Modelled Mobile Robots with Time-varying Control Barrier Functions

Jihao Huang, Zhitao Liu, Jun Zeng, Xuemin Chi, Hongye Su; arXiv:2307.08227v1 (2023). All PDF pp. 1–6 prose read; displayed equations/figures deferred.

**Task:** one unicycle navigating among known static/moving disks, using both speed and turn-rate. Rear-axle to body-center offset makes the output derivative depend on both inputs (pp. 2–4). Time-varying barriers include obstacle motion; CLF descent is relaxed in bounded-input QP, so universal goal convergence is not implied merely by having a CLF.

**Evidence:** static and two moving obstacle examples, 0.1 s simulation, average solve times 6.9/6.6 ms (pp. 4–6); no hardware, multi-robot or arbitrary polygon test. Authors explicitly leave noncircular objects and multi-robot extension open.

**DBACT use:** moving-obstacle CBF and unicycle output transformations are established. Applying them to unknown moving contact surfaces requires the missing geometry/state/feasibility premises, not just appending a time derivative.
