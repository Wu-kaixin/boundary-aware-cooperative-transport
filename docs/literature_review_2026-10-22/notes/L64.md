# L64 — Safe Adaptive Multi-Agent Coverage Control

Yang Bai, Yujie Wang, Xiaogang Xiong, Mikhail Svinin; arXiv:2306.05187v1 (2023). PDF pp. 1–6 prose read; display equations/original figures deferred. Not counted as formula-verified full reading.

**Task:** finite-size CVT coverage with unknown actuator effectiveness and bounded continuous time disturbances. Effectiveness has a known positive lower bound (not total actuator loss); nearest-neighbor position/velocity and approximation-error bounds are available (pp. 2–3). Time-invariant positive bounded density.

**Mechanism:** Fourier function approximation, projected adaptive parameter updates and augmented barrier paying parameter errors; CBF-QP enforces inter-agent separation (pp. 3–4, Theorem 1). Requires suitable initial margin and parameter-bound initialization. No sensed object-boundary or sampled-data contact guarantee.

**Evidence:** eight-agent 30 s simulation at 0.25 m safety radius, nominal versus adaptive CBF; hardware left to future work (pp. 5–6).

**DBACT use:** robust/adaptive CVT-CBF composition is established, including shared authors with other corpus work. A new density or code pipeline does not by itself establish new task capability. Cite for bounded actuation/disturbance treatment, with its assumptions rather than importing its guarantee.
