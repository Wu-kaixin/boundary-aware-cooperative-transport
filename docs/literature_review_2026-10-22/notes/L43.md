# L43 — Distributed Implementation of Control Barrier Functions for Multi-agent Systems

Xiao Tan, Dimos V. Dimarogonas; IEEE Control Systems Letters 6 (2022), starts p. 1879; DOI 10.1109/LCSYS.2021.3133802. All six PDF pages' prose read; the earlier truncation on pp. 3–4 has been repaired. Displayed equations, figures and independent proof verification remain pending.

**Capability:** solve a CBF-induced QP distributively while satisfying one coupled safety inequality at every iteration, then attain global QP optimum in finite time. Connected undirected graph, locally decomposable coefficient information; auxiliary variables are exchanged, not arbitrary no-communication operation (p. 2).

**Guarantee limits:** single coupling constraint, unbounded input and coefficient norm bounded away from zero; bounded coefficient variation and a sufficiently large adaptation gain (pp. 4–5). Multiple safety inequalities are explicitly future work. Simulation uses a global stacked-state bound, not all pairwise collision constraints (pp. 5–6).

**DBACT use:** local responsibility splitting may be conservative; exact distributed optimization has a different cost/premise set. Must not compare arbitrary pairwise half-responsibility against this paper without preserving its single-constraint setting and communication assumptions.
