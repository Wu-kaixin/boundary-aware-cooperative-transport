# L07 — Communication-free Cohesive Flexible-Object Transport using Decentralized Robot Networks

Yoshua Gombo, Anuj Tiwari, Santosh Devasia; arXiv:2102.09056v1 (2021). Full extracted text read, PDF pp. 1–6; visual verification pending.

**Capability:** reduce transient deformation of an attached flexible payload without slowing transport or explicit messages. Only leader knows destination; each robot measures own position and attachment force. Small-deformation linear springs, fixed pinned positive Laplacian and one-axis first-order position update are assumed (p. 2, (1)–(9)). Delayed self reinforcement uses current/previous force and position (p. 3, (14)). Lemma 1's Jury stability condition depends on largest pinned-Laplacian eigenvalue; its proof is omitted, not a general contact-switching theorem.

**Evidence:** four attached robots, 90 cm spring, 30 ms sample, stiffness identified offline; parameters match 10 s settling and speed limits (pp. 3–5). Seven hardware trials: maximum deformation 6.530 to 0.940 cm, reported 85% reduction (p. 6, Table I). Initial attachment and elastic deformation differ materially from unilateral pushing of an unknown rigid object. No discovery, free-space boundary deployment or collision certificate is supplied.

**DBACT use:** force feedback, physical communication and sample stability are established tools. This paper is a good model of a distinctive, measurable task capability: deformation reduction at matched settling/speed. It is contextual, not a fair direct deployment baseline.
