# L61 — Robust Control Barrier Functions for Sampled-Data Systems

Pradeep Sharma Oruganti, Parinaz Naghizadeh, Qadeer Ahmed; arXiv:2309.08050v1, 14 September 2023. Full extracted text, PDF pp. 1–9 read; formula images and proof validity not independently certified.

**Capability:** ZOH safety with bounded process disturbance and bounded state-estimate error, including matched and a special mismatched high-relative-degree case. Compact input/disturbance/error sets, smooth barriers and computable Lipschitz/reachability bounds are premises (pp. 2–4). Robust margins pay measurement, dynamics and hold-time evolution; reachability reduces conservatism (Lemma 2, (9)–(12)).

**Evidence:** unicycle acceleration example, 0.1 s hold, bounded position noise ±0.5 m and process perturbations ±0.3; 100 randomized runs (pp. 4–6). No hardware or unknown boundary reconstruction experiment.

**Critical qualification:** the theorem requires a whole-interval reachable tube; numerical example uses endpoint reachable boxes and numerically checks intermediate margins at 0.01 s increments (p. 4 footnote; pp. 8–9 Appendix D). That is a narrower numerical check, not a general equivalence of endpoint reachability and tube coverage. Table I reports h in squared-distance units; its difference must not be treated as meters of clearance. Appendix notation around norm(p) versus Lie derivative in the state bound requires checking before reuse.

**DBACT:** sampling + bounded uncertainty + CBF is prior art. A contribution must identify a new valid geometry/perception interface or measurable conservatism benefit under a precisely matched guarantee. Existing gap guard does not supply this paper's required error bounds.
