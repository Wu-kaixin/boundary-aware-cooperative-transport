# L24 — Sensor-based Multi-Robot Coverage Control with Spatial Separation in Unstructured Environments

Xinyi Wang et al.; arXiv:2403.01710v2, 10 April 2024. PDF pp. 1–7 full extracted text read; theorem implementation and original figures pending visual review. ICRA 2024 DOI in the audit is a lead, not yet publisher-verified.

Version alert: the primary arXiv record lists v3 dated 17 July 2024, with changes to the title/author list. This note describes the supplied v2 only; comparison with v3 is pending. See [verification log](../version_verification.md).

**Task:** cover information peaks in unknown 3D obstacle-rich environments. Ball robots, single-integrator dynamics, local point clouds and neighbor positions; unobserved space outside sensor footprint assumed free until updated (p. 2). Information density is a Gaussian mixture reference, not object boundary measure.

**Mechanism:** sphere flipping/convex hull and separating planes generate buffered Voronoi regions; centroid motion remains in constructed convex cells; guided cost-to-go density from voxel-map A* addresses deadlocks (pp. 3–5, (5)–(8)). Lemma 1 and Theorem 1 establish separation relative to represented obstacle points/constructed cells. They must not automatically be interpreted as a certificate for arbitrary unsampled physical surfaces. The discrete displacement must remain inside the cell; (3)'s normalized speed needs a step constraint for that premise.

**Evidence:** point-cloud forest/office simulation; robot counts 2/4/8/16 with ten trials each; comparison over 30 trials, success 96.67%, conditional successful time 15.48 s (pp. 5–6, Table I). Hardware validation is future work. The table's coverage-ratio entry 27/30 differs from success 96.67%; retain both rather than silently equate metrics.

**DBACT consequence:** local point clouds + Voronoi coverage + obstacle safety + deadlock handling already exists. Distinctiveness needs an object-interaction task or a clearly stronger information/certification interface, not generic local safe coverage.
