# L08 — Controlled Coverage Using Time-Varying Density Functions

Sung G. Lee, Magnus Egerstedt; IFAC 2013 title/DOI supplied by historical audit, publisher verification pending. PDF pp. 1–7 prose read; displayed equations/original figures deferred.

**Task:** track an externally selected smooth changing density with a CVT formation. Convex domain, first-order agents, bounded differentiable density; exact initial CVT and invertibility of I minus centroid Jacobian are central (p. 3, Theorem 1). Log concavity gives a stated sufficient invertibility condition. Full Jacobian inversion makes the controller centralized.

**Mechanism:** compensate explicit density drift and implicit Voronoi-centroid motion; proportional correction handles departures from exact CVT. Boundary movement terms matter at higher derivatives (pp. 2–3, 5).

**Evidence:** three Khepera III robots, ROS/OptiTrack, three density time constants and Lloyd/time-varying baselines (pp. 5–6). Experiments start not necessarily at a CVT, so empirical behavior is broader than exact theorem initialization, without making that premise disappear.

**DBACT use:** dynamically chosen density and centroid tracking have prior theory/hardware. This work gives neither safe unilateral object contact nor a theorem for independent inconsistent local densities; those require separate analysis.
