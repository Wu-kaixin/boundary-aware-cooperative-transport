This follows the completed contact-row decomposition and signed-motion envelope.
Those results reject the SAME original commands; they do not prove that no other
safe pushing commands exist. The full candidate execution audit remains live in

## Question and scope

Which scan sources and geometric terms create the finite-support rows that
exclude previously effective pushing commands? Separate chord/isolated-point
distance, structural half-gap, three-sigma noise allowance, physical contact
floor and radial normal. Do not attribute the entire effect to one term before
measuring the other terms. Do not tune, remove an executed safety margin or feed
simulator truth to the controller.

Keep the original four completed control trajectories and their preselected
first/peak positive-force events: circle frames 79/162, L 71/124, C 478/572,
skew box 559/891. Retain all 128 states, including the 16 nonadmissions. The
primary force subset remains all 17 states, with the same 11 declared pushers.
These are correlated development observations, not new independent trials.

## Fixed-row algebra and evaluation-only geometry

For each retained row report

    r = n.u + gamma*(distance-r_safe) - gamma*structural
        - gamma*noise - rho - received_speed.

Keep every row, not only the prior limiting one. For each state record the prior
deterministic limiting row and recompute minima over the SAME rows with only the
structural term, only the noise term, or both terms omitted. These are algebraic
attributions, not proposed controllers, proof that an allowance is unnecessary,
or simulations of changed row-admission rules. Keep the original motion term.
Retain the previously established signed-ball witness flags as fixed subgroups.

After the original control replay has passed, use saved pre-command cargo
vertices to evaluate signed surface distance, closest point and outward normal.
Compare the observed lower distance and normal with this evaluation geometry.
The positive contact force belongs to the ensuing recorded control interval;
do not imply it is simultaneous with this pre-command geometry. Never feed these
evaluation values back into any control or counterfactual solver.

Report all 128 states and all retained feature rows, then the same 17-state and
11-state subgroup counts. Separate own versus one-hop limiting sources,
chord versus isolated-point features, geometric terms and remaining conflicts.
Nonadmissions keep explicit guard reasons. No statistical significance or
population reliability claim is licensed by these fixed development states.

## Provenance and next decision

Before execution, snapshot this design, the new driver, its existing helper
sources, predecessor hashes and runtime/protocol identity in a fresh directory
under runs/research. Preserve every failed attempt if any. Verify frozen source,
environment, helpers and best configuration before and after replay.

Interpret the result together with the still-pending full trajectory audit.
Any future mechanism change needs its own design, must retain the best numerical
parameters, and must protect legitimate pushing as well as resolve false
geometry conflicts. This diagnostic alone cannot authorize candidate adoption.
